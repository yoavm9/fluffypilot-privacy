Fluffy Pilot Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Fluffy Pilot (the "App") handles, and why. The App is made by an independent developer ("we", "us").

Information we collect

We don't ask for, collect or store any personal information such as your name, email address or contacts. The App has no accounts or sign-in. The App uses Google services for advertising and analytics, described below.

Your game progress (high score, coins, unlocked plane colors and settings) is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us, and deleting the App deletes it.

Advertising

The App shows optional rewarded video ads (for example, watching an ad to get an extra life). These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, Google may automatically collect information from your device, such as:

  • device and app identifiers (on iOS, the advertising identifier is only available if you allow tracking; on Android, the advertising ID unless you've deleted it)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • device type, operating system and language
  • how you interact with ads (for example, views and taps)
  • diagnostic information such as ad load errors

This information is collected and handled by Google under its own policies. We don't receive it in a form that identifies you. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Analytics

To understand how the game is played and make it better, the App uses Google Analytics for Firebase. It records gameplay events such as rounds started and finished, scores, how a round ended, tutorial progress, plane colors bought or chosen, and whether an optional ad was watched. Along with these events, Firebase automatically collects:

  • an app instance ID (a random identifier for this installation of the App)
  • device model, operating system, language and approximate location (country or region)
  • session information, such as when and how long the App is used
  • the advertising identifier where it is available, as described above

We use this information only in aggregate, to improve the game. It is never used to identify you and never sold. Learn more in Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): if you're in one of these regions, the App asks for your consent before ads and analytics use your data. You can change your choice at any time from Settings > Privacy in the App.
  • iOS tracking: you can turn off tracking for all apps in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Ads will still appear but won't be personalized.
  • Android advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Settings > Privacy > Ads (on some devices, Settings > Google > Ads). Ads will still appear but won't be personalized.
  • Ads are optional: you never have to watch an ad to play the game.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us and we'll help remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its use. Since we don't hold personal information ourselves, requests about advertising data are best directed to Google, but you're welcome to contact us and we'll help.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
